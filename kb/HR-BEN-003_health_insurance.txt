[HR-BEN-003 Section 1 - Who is covered]
All permanent employees are covered from their first day of work. Spouses and children (under 20, or under 23 if studying full-time) can be added at the employee's cost at the group rate. Enrolment for dependants is open in January each year or within 30 days of marriage or birth.

[HR-BEN-003 Section 2 - Plan tiers and coverage limits]
Plan is assigned by job level.
Plan Silver (job levels P1-P3):
- IPD (inpatient) room and board: up to 3,000 THB per night, max 365 days per year
- IPD medical expenses: up to 100,000 THB per admission
- OPD (outpatient): up to 1,500 THB per visit, 30 visits per year, annual OPD cap 30,000 THB
- Dental: 3,000 THB per year
- Annual health check-up: basic package
Plan Gold (job levels P4-P5 and M1):
- IPD room and board: up to 5,000 THB per night
- IPD medical expenses: up to 200,000 THB per admission
- OPD: up to 2,000 THB per visit, 30 visits per year, annual OPD cap 40,000 THB
- Dental: 5,000 THB per year
- Annual health check-up: executive package
Plan Platinum (job levels M2 and above):
- IPD room and board: up to 8,000 THB per night
- IPD medical expenses: up to 400,000 THB per admission
- OPD: up to 3,000 THB per visit, 40 visits per year, annual OPD cap 60,000 THB
- Dental: 8,000 THB per year
- Eyeglasses / contact lenses: 3,000 THB per year
- Annual health check-up: executive plus package
All plans include: accident coverage (OPD within 24 hours), ambulance, and life & accident insurance of 24 times monthly salary (max 2,000,000 THB).

[HR-BEN-003 Section 3 - How to claim: network hospital (cashless)]
Use this when you visit one of the 400+ SafeCare network hospitals (for example Bangkok Hospital, Samitivej, BNH, Phyathai, Vejthani - demo list).
1. Show your SafeCare e-card in the SafeCare app (or physical card) and your identity document at registration (see Section 11 for exactly what to bring).
2. The hospital checks your coverage directly with SafeCare.
3. You pay nothing for covered items. You pay only the amount above your plan limit and non-covered items (e.g. vitamins, cosmetic treatment).
4. For planned IPD admissions, ask the hospital to request pre-approval (Fax claim) at least 3 days before admission.

[HR-BEN-003 Section 4 - How to claim: non-network hospital or clinic (reimbursement)]
1. Pay the bill yourself.
2. Collect: (a) original official receipt in your name, (b) medical certificate showing diagnosis, (c) itemised bill for IPD, (d) copy of your ID card, (e) bank book page for transfer.
3. Submit within 30 days of the treatment date (IPD: within 90 days) through the SafeCare app (Claims > New claim > upload photos) OR give the documents to the Benefits Desk, 25th Floor.
4. SafeCare reviews the claim within 15 working days and transfers the money to your bank account. You can track status in the SafeCare app.
5. Claims submitted after the deadline may be rejected.

[HR-BEN-003 Section 5 - Dental claims]
Dental treatment (cleaning, filling, extraction, X-ray) is reimbursed up to your plan's yearly dental limit. Submit the official receipt and dental certificate through the SafeCare app within 30 days. Orthodontics (braces) and cosmetic whitening are not covered.

[HR-BEN-003 Section 6 - Annual health check-up]
Book your annual check-up between March and November at a network hospital listed in the People Portal > Benefits > Health check. Show your e-card; no payment is needed for the package. Results are confidential and sent only to you.

[HR-BEN-003 Section 7 - Not covered]
Pre-existing conditions in the first 120 days (except for employees transferred from the previous policy), cosmetic surgery, infertility treatment, self-inflicted injuries, alternative medicine not prescribed by a doctor, and treatment outside Thailand unless it is an emergency during an approved business trip.

[HR-BEN-003 Section 8 - Social Security]
In addition to group insurance, all employees are registered with the Social Security Office (SSO). Employees contribute 5% of salary (max 750 THB per month). SSO hospital selection can be changed once a year between January and March.

[HR-BEN-003 Section 11 - Documents to bring to a network hospital]
At registration, every visit (OPD or IPD), bring:
1. Your SafeCare member card - the e-card in the SafeCare app or the physical card.
2. Your original identity document:
   - Thai employees: Thai national ID card (บัตรประชาชน).
   - Non-Thai employees: passport.
   Photocopies and photos of the card on your phone are not accepted.
For dependants (spouse, children) on your policy:
   - Spouse: their Thai national ID card or passport, plus their SafeCare e-card.
   - Children under 7 (no ID card yet): birth certificate (สูติบัตร) or passport, plus their SafeCare e-card.
For a planned admission or surgery: also bring the doctor's appointment letter; the hospital requests SafeCare pre-approval at least 3 days before admission (Section 3).
If you forget your card or ID: the hospital may ask you to pay first. Keep the original receipt and medical certificate and claim reimbursement within 30 days (Section 4).
