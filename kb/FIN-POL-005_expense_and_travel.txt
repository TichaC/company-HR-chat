DOCUMENT: FIN-POL-005 Expense Claims and Business Travel Policy
OWNER: Finance | VERSION: 2026.1 | EFFECTIVE: 1 January 2026
NOTE: Demo data prepared for an internal AI Coworker demonstration.

[FIN-POL-005 Section 1 - How to submit an expense claim]
1. Open the BE8 Expense app > New claim.
2. Select the project code (client work) or cost centre (internal).
3. Take a photo of each receipt. Official tax invoices must show the company name "Beryl 8 Plus Co., Ltd." and tax ID.
4. Submit within 30 days of the expense date. Your manager approves, then Finance reviews.
5. Approved claims are paid with your next salary if approved by the 15th of the month, otherwise the following month.

[FIN-POL-005 Section 2 - Local transport]
- Taxi / Grab for client visits: actual cost with receipt or app e-receipt.
- Using your own car: 6 THB per kilometre plus tolls and parking with receipts.
- Late-night work after 22:00: taxi home is reimbursable.

[FIN-POL-005 Section 3 - Meals and client entertainment]
- Overtime meal (working after 20:00): up to 200 THB.
- Client entertainment: must be pre-approved by the practice head; record attendee names and purpose.

[FIN-POL-005 Section 4 - Domestic travel (outside Bangkok)]
- Per diem: 500 THB per day.
- Hotel: up to 2,500 THB per night.
- Flights: economy class, booked through the Finance travel desk at least 7 days ahead.

[FIN-POL-005 Section 5 - International travel]
- Per diem: 60 USD per day (Asia), 90 USD per day (other regions).
- Hotel: up to 150 USD per night (Asia), 220 USD per night (other regions).
- Flights: economy for trips under 6 hours, premium economy for longer flights.
- Travel insurance is provided automatically; request a certificate from finance@beryl8-demo.com.

[FIN-POL-005 Section 6 - Contacts]
Finance: finance@beryl8-demo.com, ext. 3100.
